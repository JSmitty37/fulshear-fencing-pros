Common Questions

Fence FAQ for Fulshear Homeowners

Real answers to the questions we hear every week from Fulshear, Cross Creek Ranch, and Weston Lakes homeowners.

A new fence installation in Fulshear typically ranges from $2,500 to $8,500 depending on material, height, and linear footage. Cedar wood privacy fences average $18–$25 per linear foot installed. Ornamental iron runs $28–$45 per foot. Chain link is the most affordable at $8–$15 per foot installed. We provide free written estimates with no obligation so you know the exact number before committing.

Cedar is the most popular choice for Fulshear homeowners because it naturally resists moisture, insects, and the intense heat of a Houston-area summer. It weathers beautifully and can be stained to match any aesthetic. For zero-maintenance durability, vinyl or powder-coated ornamental iron are excellent alternatives. We do not recommend untreated pine for ground-contact applications in Fort Bend County — the humidity and clay soil conditions accelerate rot.

Yes. The City of Fulshear requires a fence permit for most new fence installations — including rear and side yard applications and any fence over 30 inches in a front yard setback. We handle the permit application process on your behalf at no additional charge. Most residential permits are approved within 3–7 business days.

Almost certainly yes if you live in Cross Creek Ranch, Weston Lakes, Fulbrook on Fulshear Creek, Del Webb Sweetgrass, or Polo Ranch. Each of these HOAs requires Architectural Review Committee (ARC) approval before installation — covering style, height, material, and color. We are familiar with each community's requirements and will advise you on compliant options before you submit your application. We can also help you prepare the application packet.

Most residential fence installations in Fulshear take 1–3 days. Larger projects over 200 linear feet, or lots with significant grade changes along the Brazos River corridor, may take 3–5 business days. After your estimate is approved, we typically schedule installation within 1–2 weeks.

In Fort Bend County's clay-heavy soil, we set posts to a minimum depth of 24 inches for a standard 6-foot privacy fence — deeper than the national industry standard. We concrete every post and use proper drainage practices to prevent heaving and leaning during the wet season. This is one of the most important reasons our fences outlast competitors' work.

For most dogs, a 6-foot cedar or vinyl privacy fence is ideal — it provides containment and eliminates the visual stimulation that triggers fence-running and attempted jumping. For determined escape artists, we recommend a board-on-board design with no horizontal rails on the interior side (no "step ladder" effect). We discuss your specific dogs' size and behavior during the free estimate to recommend the right solution.
