Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Overview

Fulshear Fencing Pros ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website at fulshearfencingpros.com. This privacy policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use it, and how you can contact us with questions or requests.

We take your privacy seriously. We do not sell your information to third parties. We do not spam. The information you submit through our website is used only to respond to your fence estimate request.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you submit a contact or estimate request form on this website. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • ZIP code or community name
  • Project description and service interest

We also collect non-personally-identifiable information automatically through standard web analytics tools, including pages visited, time on page, browser type, and referring URL. This data is used in aggregate to improve the website and understand how visitors find us.

How We Use Your Information

Information you submit through our forms is used to:

  • Contact you to discuss your fence project and schedule a free estimate
  • Provide you with the requested services
  • Follow up on your project if needed

We do not use your contact information for unsolicited marketing, and we do not sell or rent it to other businesses.

Form Processing — Formspree

Our website contact forms are processed by Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form handling service. When you submit a form, your data is transmitted to Formspree's servers and then forwarded to us. Formspree's privacy policy governs their handling of this data. We encourage you to review their policy at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Analytics

This website may use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data (pages viewed, session duration, traffic sources). This data does not identify individual users. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Cookies

This website may set cookies for analytics and performance purposes. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; however, doing so may affect website functionality. We do not use cookies for advertising targeting or cross-site tracking.

Data Retention

We retain contact information submitted through our forms for as long as is reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and provide the requested services. We do not maintain long-term marketing databases. If you would like us to delete your information, contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through this website, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

Lead Generation Disclosure

This website is operated as a lead generation property for fence contracting services in the Fulshear, TX area. When you submit a quote request, your information is received by the fence contractor(s) serving this area who will contact you to schedule your estimate. You are not charged for submitting a request.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at the phone number or address below. We will respond to verifiable requests within a reasonable time.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
