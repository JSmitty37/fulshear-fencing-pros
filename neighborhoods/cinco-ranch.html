Serving Cinco Ranch from Our Fulshear Base

Cinco Ranch is West Houston's most established master-planned community — developed since the early 1990s along Mason Road and FM 1093 in Katy. With thousands of homes now 15–25 years old, Cinco Ranch has significant demand for both fence replacement (aging original fences) and storm damage repair (mature tree canopy plus Houston's annual storm season).

From our Fulshear base, Cinco Ranch is a 10–15 minute drive via FM 1093. We serve all Cinco Ranch villages — North, South, Southwest, and the Kelliwood and Firethorne adjacent areas — at no travel surcharge. Same pricing, same crew quality, same warranty as our primary Fulshear service area.

What Cinco Ranch Homeowners Need Most

Full Fence Replacement

Many original Cinco Ranch fences are now 15–20+ years old and past the point of economical repair. We replace aging fences with new cedar board-on-board, matching HOA requirements and using deeper post footings than the originals.

Storm Damage Repair

Gate Repair & Replacement

Sagging gates, failed latches, broken hinges — gate hardware wears out faster than fence panels. We repair or replace walk gates and driveway gates and can add automated openers to existing gate systems.

Aging Fences in Cinco Ranch — Repair vs. Replace

With original fences now 15–25 years old across large parts of Cinco Ranch, replacement decisions come up regularly. Our honest framework:

ConditionRecommendationWhy
1–3 damaged sections, rest of fence solidRepairIsolated damage doesn't justify full replacement cost
Posts leaning throughout, boards still OKRepair (post replacement)New posts extend life 10+ years if boards are sound
40%+ of boards rotted or warpedReplacePatching aging boards is costly relative to full replacement
Multiple posts failing + boards weatheredReplaceRepairing foundation and surface together costs near replacement price
Fence 15+ years old, post rot startingReplaceYou're near end of useful life — invest in new fence now, not repeated repairs

We tell you honestly which situation you're in at your free estimate. We don't push replacement when repair serves you better.
