Popular Fence Styles in Weston Lakes

Cedar Board-on-Board Privacy

The standard backyard solution on large estate lots. 6-ft height, set on 4×4 posts at 36–42-inch depth for soil stability. Stained to complement the home's exterior color palette.

Ornamental Aluminum

Most popular for front-facing and golf-course-side perimeters. Black powder-coated panels, 4 ft or custom height. Low maintenance — critical for large properties where repainting is a significant recurring cost.

Wrought Iron Estate Entry

For properties with a formal entrance aesthetic. Double swing driveway gates in wrought iron or heavy-gauge steel, often paired with brick or stucco columns. Requires annual rust inspection and touch-up painting in coastal humidity.

Pool Barrier Fencing

Aluminum pool barrier fencing to Texas code (48-inch minimum, self-closing self-latching gate). Many Weston Lakes homes have pools — code compliance is non-negotiable and must be permitted through Fort Bend County.

Storm Damage & Brazos River Properties

Properties in and around Weston Lakes face a specific storm risk profile: the Brazos River can swell dramatically after major rain events, saturating soil for extended periods. Saturated clay loses its grip on fence posts, and the weight of wet panels creates additional lateral pressure. After Hurricane Harvey, Tropical Storm Beta, and Hurricane Beryl, Weston Lakes fence repair calls spike significantly.

We respond quickly after major storm events and provide insurance documentation packages — written damage assessments, itemized estimates, and photographic evidence — that your homeowner's insurance adjuster can use to process claims. Contact us as soon after a storm as possible, as documentation quality is best when damage is fresh.
