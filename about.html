About Fulshear Fencing Pros

Locally owned, locally operated. We live in the same HOA-governed communities we serve — and we build fences we'd want on our own property.

Our Story

Built for Fulshear Homeowners, By Someone Who Lives Here

Fulshear Fencing Pros was built on a straightforward premise: homeowners in Cross Creek Ranch, Weston Lakes, and Fulbrook deserve a fence contractor who actually understands their community — not a franchise crew from out of town that treats every job the same regardless of HOA rules, clay soil conditions, or hurricane season timing.

We specialize in the specific challenges of Fort Bend County fencing: the expansive black clay that destroys shallow post installations, the HOA ARC processes that require proper documentation and patience, and the storm season demands that put every fence in the area through an annual stress test.

When you hire us, you're working directly with the people doing the work — not a call center routing your job to whoever's available. That accountability is the whole point.

What We Stand For

Our Commitments to Every Customer

Honest Repair vs. Replace Advice

We won't recommend replacement when repair will do. We've turned down full replacement jobs that didn't need it — because our reputation in this community matters more than any single project's revenue.

No HOA Violations — Ever

We manage the ARC submission process and don't start work without written approval. Zero HOA violations on our record. We won't rush a job and put your property at risk of a mandatory removal.

Built for This Climate

Fort Bend County's clay soil and Gulf Coast storm season require specific engineering decisions — post depth, concrete mix, hardware specifications. We don't cut corners where corners matter.

Transparent Pricing

Itemized estimates in writing before any work begins. The price we quote is the price you pay — no hidden add-ons discovered on installation day.

We Answer the Phone

After a storm, when you need a quote, when you have a question about your ARC submission — we pick up. If we miss a call, we return it same business day.

Credentials

Licensed, Insured & Accountable

Texas Contractor License

Licensed to operate as a general contractor in the State of Texas. License documentation available upon request for HOA submission packages.

General Liability Insurance

Fully insured with general liability coverage. Certificate of insurance available for all projects — required by many HOA ARC submissions and useful for homeowner records.

Workers' Compensation

Our crews are covered. You are not liable for on-site injuries when you hire a properly insured contractor. Don't hire crews that can't provide WC documentation.

Fort Bend County Permits

We pull permits for all projects that require them — pool barrier fencing, large commercial installs. We don't ask homeowners to pull their own permits, which is a red flag when contractors request it.

Ask any contractor for proof of license and insurance before work begins. A contractor who hesitates or can't produce documentation is uninsured — and if something goes wrong, you're liable.
