What to Do After Storm Damage to Your Fence

Fulshear sits in Hurricane Beryl territory. After every major Gulf Coast storm — tropical storms, category 1-3 hurricanes, severe thunderstorms, and even high-wind events that don't make the news — we receive calls from Fulshear, Cross Creek Ranch, Weston Lakes, and Pecan Grove homeowners with downed fences, blown panels, and snapped posts.

The most important thing you can do after fence storm damage is document before you touch anything. Photos taken before debris is moved significantly strengthen your insurance claim. Then call us — we'll assess the damage, provide insurance-ready documentation, and get your fence repaired or replaced quickly.

Your Step-by-Step Storm Response Checklist

  1. 1
    Photograph everything first

    Before moving debris or touching the fence, take multiple photos from different angles. Include wide shots showing the full damage extent and close-ups of individual sections. This documentation is critical for your insurance claim.

  2. 2
    Secure safety hazards

    If fallen sections have created a safety hazard — open pool access, property security gap, sharp debris — make the minimal intervention needed for safety. Otherwise, leave everything in place for the insurance adjuster visit.

  3. 3
    Call your homeowner's insurance

    Open a claim as soon as possible. Note your claim number. Ask about your deductible and what "other structures" coverage applies. In Texas, wind damage from storms is typically covered under HO policies — flood damage is separate.

  4. 4
    Call us for a damage assessment

    Call (832) 734-9878 for a same-week damage assessment. We provide written documentation, photos, and an itemized estimate in the format insurance adjusters require. We can also communicate directly with your adjuster.

  5. 5
    Schedule repair or replacement

    Once your claim is approved and materials are confirmed, we schedule the repair or full replacement. Emergency situations (pool exposure, security gaps) get priority scheduling.

Types of Storm Damage We Repair

Blown fence panels

High winds from tropical systems lift and carry entire sections of privacy fencing. Post replacement and full panel reinstallation.

Snapped and cracked posts

Direct impact from falling trees or extreme wind loading causes post failure at ground level. New concrete-set posts at deeper depth.

Tree-impact damage

Neighbor trees or your own trees falling across the fence line. Coordination with tree services for debris removal + full fence restoration.

Complete fence loss

Total fence loss from major storm events. We provide full replacement estimates for insurance claims and rebuild to current HOA specifications.
