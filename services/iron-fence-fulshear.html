Lasting Elegance for Fulshear Estates

Ornamental Fencing Built for West Houston's Climate

When curb appeal, security, and elegance matter more than privacy, ornamental iron and aluminum fencing is the Fulshear homeowner's answer. It's the fence that defines the front of an estate, frames a pool deck, or adds architectural character to a premium property without blocking the yard's landscaping from view.

The challenge in Fulshear's Gulf Coast climate is corrosion. Traditional bare wrought iron rusts aggressively in our humidity without regular maintenance. That's why we install powder-coated steel and aluminum alternatives that look identical to wrought iron but perform far better in Fort Bend County's environment. Powder coating creates a factory-applied electrostatic bond that is orders of magnitude more durable than brush-on paint — providing a rust-resistant, UV-stable finish that lasts decades.

Pool Fence Code Requirements in Fulshear, TX

Texas pool barrier code (Chapter 757, Texas Health & Safety Code) requires:

  • →Minimum barrier height of 48 inches on all sides
  • →No openings more than 4 inches in any dimension
  • →No footholds or handholds on the pool side of the fence
  • →Self-latching, self-closing gate that opens away from the pool

Our ornamental aluminum pool fence designs are engineered to meet all Texas and Fort Bend County pool barrier requirements. We will help you obtain any required permits.
