Texas Pool Barrier Gate Requirements

Texas State Pool Gate Code (IRC Section AG105)

  • Barrier height: Minimum 48 inches from finished grade
  • Openings: Must not allow a 4-inch sphere to pass through
  • Self-closing: Gate must automatically close and latch after each use
  • Self-latching: Latch must engage without human assistance
  • Latch location: Pool-side of the gate at 54"+ from grade, OR either side if located within 3" of gate top
  • Opening direction: Gate must open outward, away from pool
  • Fort Bend County: Permits required for new pools — barrier inspection included

We build every pool gate to exceed these requirements. Non-compliant pool barriers expose homeowners to liability — don't cut corners on pool gate specs.

HOA Gate Approval in Fulshear Subdivisions

Adding a gate in Cross Creek Ranch, Weston Lakes, Fulbrook on Fulshear Creek, or Polo Ranch requires ARC approval before installation begins. We've navigated gate approvals across Fulshear's major HOA communities and know what most committees require:

  • Material match: Gate material must match or complement the fence material and color approved for your lot
  • Height restriction: Most communities limit driveway gates to the height of the adjacent fence (typically 6 ft)
  • Post size: Some ARCs specify minimum post sizes for gate columns (4×4 or 6×6 brick column) for aesthetic consistency
  • Automated hardware visibility: Some communities restrict visible operators — we can recommend concealed or low-profile operators when needed
  • Setback requirements: Gates must typically be set back from the front property line per Fort Bend County zoning

We provide dimensioned gate drawings for your ARC submission at no additional charge. Most Fulshear ARC committees review applications in 2–4 weeks.
