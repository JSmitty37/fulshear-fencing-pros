## PART 1 — MARKET SELECTION RATIONALE

### Why Fencing?
Fencing is one of the most Facebook-friendly home services niches available:
- Avg job value: $4,000–$8,000 (high enough that contractors will pay $750–$1,000/mo for exclusive leads)
- Visual service = carousel and video ads convert exceptionally well
- Multiple emotional triggers: privacy, pets, pool safety, curb appeal, new home
- Year-round demand in Texas (no winter shutdowns)
- Facebook CPL averages $22–$25 nationally — lower than roofing
- Low digital sophistication from most local fence contractors = easy to add value

### Why McKinney, TX?
McKinney sits inside the Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex, the single strongest fencing market in the country:

| Signal | Data |
|---|---|
| Population | ~200,000 (McKinney proper) |
| Metro Growth | DFW leads the nation — 4 of top 5 fastest-growing cities nationally are DFW suburbs |
| Regional Market Share | South U.S. = 40.7% of national fencing market |
| Avg Fence Job | $4,000–$8,000 (wood privacy, vinyl, ornamental iron) |
| Homeownership | ~65%+ suburban TX homeowners |
| Climate | Year-round outdoor season |
| Facebook CPL (Fencing) | $22–$25 national avg; Texas markets slightly lower |
| Digital Competition | Most local fence contractors have weak or no digital marketing |

Collin County (McKinney's county) is one of the fastest-growing counties in the United States, with massive new housing construction adding thousands of fenceable properties per year. The audience targeting on Facebook for homeowners aged 30–65 in this area is enormous and highly affordable.

## PART 3 — 10-STEP LAUNCH PLAYBOOK

### Step 1: Market Selection ✅ DONE
McKinney, TX — Fencing. Confirmed.

### Step 2: Demand Validation
Owner: Jackson
- Check Google search volume for "fence company McKinney TX," "fence installation Frisco TX," etc.
- Audit the Google Maps pack (look for 3–10 local fence companies — confirms contractors exist to sell to)
- Check Angi for McKinney fencing — count how many contractors are actively listed (buying leads)
- Check Facebook Ad Library for any fence companies running ads in DFW — if few/none, CPL will be low

### Step 3: Website Built
Owner: Jackson
- Domain target: mckinneyfencepros.com (or collincopros.com, dallasmetrofence.com)
- Clone the sandyroofingpros.com architecture
- Swap niche content: roofing → fencing
- Swap location: Salt Lake County → Collin County / McKinney
- Set up: CallRail TX number, Formspree form, GitHub Pages hosting, Meta Pixel
- Fence-specific page sections: Privacy Fence, Wood Fence, Vinyl Fence, Iron Fence, Pool Fence, Free Estimate

### Step 4: Build BO (Business Owner) List
Owner: Noah
- Target: 10–15 fence contractors in McKinney / Collin County
- Sources: Google Maps "fence company McKinney TX," Angi, Yelp, Facebook
- Prioritize:
  1. Contractors actively on Angi/HomeAdvisor (already in "buy leads" mode — easiest pitch)
  2. Small local ops with < 50 Google reviews (hungry, most to gain)
  3. Skip: Top 5-star shops with 200+ reviews (self-sufficient, won't need you)
- For each: collect owner name, direct phone, email, service focus, review count

### Step 5: Outreach — Get a BO Committed
Owner: Noah

**Opening Script:**
"Hey, is this [Name]? My name is Noah — I help run a local fencing lead site here in McKinney. Do you have like 60 seconds?"

**The offer:**
"We just launched mckinneyfencepros.com and we're starting to get homeowners requesting free fence estimates. We're looking to work with one or two local contractors on a trial basis — completely free leads to start, no commitment. Would that be something you'd be open to?"

**If yes:**
"Perfect. What's the best number to text you leads on? One thing I'll need — if we send you a lead, can you commit to calling them back within an hour? That's all we ask."

**Key Objections:**
- "How much does it cost?" → "Nothing right now. Free trial. If the leads convert we talk about a monthly arrangement — zero risk to you."
- "I use Angi/HomeAdvisor" → "That's why I called — those leads go to 4 other contractors. Ours go to you alone. No bidding war."
- "I'm too busy" → "That's actually the best time to set it up so you have backup coming in when things slow down."

### Step 6: Launch Meta Ads
Owner: Jackson

**Campaign Setup:**
- Ad Account: Create new "McKinneyFencePros" account
- Objective: Leads (Advantage+ audience)
- Budget: $15/day to start
- Duration: Run for 7 days minimum before evaluating

**Targeting:**
- Location: McKinney, TX + 15-mile radius (covers Frisco, Allen, Plano, Celina)
- Age: 30–65
- Demographics: Homeowners, household income $75K+
- Interests: Home improvement, backyard living, pets, outdoor space
- Exclude: Likely renters

**Ad Creative — Launch with 2 Campaigns:**

Campaign A — Privacy Angle ($7.50/day)
- Headline: "Get the Backyard Privacy You've Been Putting Off"
- Body: "McKinney homeowners — stop looking at your neighbor's yard. Local fence pros install wood, vinyl & iron privacy fences fast. Free estimates, no obligation."
- Creative: Before/after carousel (old fence → new wood privacy fence)
- CTA: "Get Free Estimate" → Lead Form

Campaign B — Pet Safety Angle ($7.50/day)
- Headline: "Keep Your Dogs Safe — Local Fence Pros in McKinney"
- Body: "Let your dogs run free. We install custom wood, vinyl, and chain link fences for Collin County homeowners. Fast installs. Free quotes."
- Creative: Photo of dog in fenced backyard
- CTA: "Get Free Estimate" → Lead Form

**Kill Rule:** After $75 spend + 72 hrs: CPL > $55 → kill that creative
**Scale Rule:** CPL < $20 → double the daily budget on that campaign

### Step 7: Evaluate Ads (~$100 spend mark)
Owner: Jackson
- Review at ~$100 total spend
- Which angle won? Privacy or pets?
- CPL under $30 = healthy for fencing
- CPL $30–$55 = acceptable, monitor
- CPL > $55 = kill and retest creative
- Launch 2 new creative variations based on what performed

### Step 8: Handle Leads → Send to BO
Owner: Jackson → Noah
- Lead comes in via Formspree → triggers Make.com scenario
- Creates Notion row in Live Leads database
- Sends Gmail alert to Jackson within 60 seconds
- Jackson texts Noah: "Name | Phone | [McKinney/Frisco/etc.] | Fence type"
- Noah dials within 15 minutes

### Step 9: Free Trial — Monitor BO Response
Owner: Noah
- Track: Did the contractor call the lead back within 1 hour?
- If yes → they're a good fit, push toward retainer
- If no or slow → address it directly ("The leads are only valuable if you respond fast — are you able to commit to that?")
- Collect feedback: What type of leads are most valuable? Privacy fence? Pool? Wood vs vinyl?

### Step 10: Close BO — Monthly Retainer
Owner: Noah

**Pitch after 3–5 delivered leads:**
"You've gotten X leads from us over the past two weeks. These are exclusive — no other contractor in your zone gets them. To keep them coming, we're moving to $750/month for your territory. That's one job and you've more than covered it. Want to lock in your territory before someone else does?"

**Retainer Pricing:**
- Territory 1 (McKinney Core): $750–$1,000/mo
- Territories 2–5: $600–$800/mo each
- Full site revenue at 5 territories: $3,500–$4,600/mo

**Contract:** Use the same Stripe + simple retainer agreement from the SLC Roofing model.
